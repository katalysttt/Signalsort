Interviewer: How often do you use the app?
James: Two or three times a week, mostly dinner.
Interviewer: What do you like about it?
James: The live tracking is great, I love seeing exactly where the rider is.
Interviewer: What frustrates you?
James: I cannot find my previous orders. I wanted a receipt for work and spent ages looking for it. In the end I used search but it still wasn't obvious.
Interviewer: And checkout?
James: Payment selection is confusing. I'm never sure which card is selected, so I open and close the payment screen to double check.
